oxgl test results

Oxgl is headless WebGL 1 and WebGL 2 for Node.js that strives for standards compliance. It renders through ANGLE. Its CI runs the Khronos WebGL conformance suites and three.js's own examples; each section below says where. This page shows every test's result on each platform from one CI run.

Results that are known and accepted are recorded with a reason in conformance/expectations.txt and threejs/expectations.txt. Hover over a result that isn't PASS to see why: the expectation's reason, the failure message, or "not run" when a platform has no result. A result marked with ! wasn't expected on its platform; those are the results that make CI fail.

Generated by scripts/results-page.mjs from the CI run's reports. Source and issues: github.com/oxgl-js/oxgl.
