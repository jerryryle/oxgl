## 1. Constant tables

- [ ] 1.1 Write `scripts/webgl-constants.mjs`. It fetches `specs/latest/1.0/webgl.idl` and `specs/latest/2.0/webgl2.idl` at the commit in `conformance/suite.json` and writes `src/generated/webgl-constants.ts`: `WEBGL1_CONSTANTS` from the `WebGLRenderingContextBase` mixin and `WEBGL2_ONLY_CONSTANTS` from `WebGL2RenderingContextBase`, both `as const`, and `WEBGL2_CONSTANTS` as their spread.
